Mission & operating constraints

I developed a vision-guided landing pipeline for GPS-denied quadrotor operations in PX4 SITL and Gazebo. The task connected perception, coordinate transformations, obstacle avoidance, and flight-control setpoints into repeatable simulated landing behavior.

The résumé reports landing performance across more than 70 simulation runs. The existing portfolio separately documents a LiDAR warehouse-navigation implementation. Both are described here as related autonomy work; the warehouse controller parameters are not presented as the exact configuration used in every landing benchmark.

My responsibility & architecture

  • Built the vision-guided autonomous landing pipeline and improved the LiDAR/vision perception stack.
  • Redesigned obstacle avoidance and path planning, then smoothed velocity setpoints to eliminate trajectory oscillation.
  • Resolved NED/ENU frame inconsistencies and demonstrated stable 2 m hover under simulated crosswind disturbances.

In the existing LiDAR implementation, a 50 Hz reactive controller combines a goal-line attractor with five-sector obstacle avoidance. A slower 0.2 Hz A* planner supplies visualization and recovery reference, rather than primary flight commands.

Engineering decisions & tradeoffs

The LiDAR processing node extracts a horizontal slice, filters nearby self-returns, clamps infinite readings, and produces a 2D scan. The mapping implementation replaced a crashing hector_mapping setup with Bresenham ray casting on a 320 × 320 grid at 5 cm resolution. That covers a 16 m × 16 m field.

The important tradeoff is close-range visibility versus self-return rejection. A 0.45 m exclusion radius must be considered alongside the controller’s documented 0.55 m soft-stop distance. Those thresholds describe the simulation configuration; they do not establish a physical vehicle safety margin.

Separating fast avoidance from slow planning avoids making immediate obstacle response wait on a global map update. The cost is that reactive behavior needs careful arbitration to prevent competing steering terms.
