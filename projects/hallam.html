Engineering problem & facility context

Semiconductor and research facilities need utility layouts that connect equipment requirements to coordinated construction documents. At Hallam-ICS, I developed fabrication-ready layouts and mechanical construction drawings with senior engineers.

The engineering constraints included equipment interfaces, code-compliant system design, and consistency between drawings, P&IDs, and equipment documentation. The supplied résumé does not provide facility floor areas, flow rates, loads, or project budgets.

My responsibility

  • Designed layouts and construction drawings using Revit and AutoCAD.
  • Developed utility layouts for process gas, cooling water, and exhaust.
  • Supported P&ID and equipment cut-sheet reviews.
  • Coordinated with senior engineers through design reviews and contributed to commissioning documentation.

Design workflow & interfaces

01Equipment cut sheets / P&IDs
02Coordinated utility layouts
03Drawing reviews / commissioning documentation

Equipment documentation defines the connections a layout must accommodate. Drawings communicate routing and installation intent, while P&IDs describe process relationships. Coordinating those views is central to producing usable facility documents.

The résumé supports layout and review work. It does not document personal ownership of stamped design, final code approval, or completed HVAC load calculations.

Closing an equipment-to-utility discrepancy

An equipment revision changed a utility connection requirement, leaving the equipment cut sheet and the layout/P&ID out of agreement. I traced the discrepancy back to the revised equipment documentation and reviewed its impact on the utility layout with the senior engineer.

I revised the affected drawing information and coordinated the connection requirements across the design documents. The correction was checked through the design-review process so the construction information reflected the current equipment interface.

Review & deliverables

The documented deliverables are fabrication-ready layouts, construction drawings, and contributions to commissioning documentation. Design reviews with senior engineers supported the work. No installed-system performance or commissioning pass rate is claimed.

Engineering takeaway: layout quality depends on agreement between equipment requirements and the documents used to install and commission the system.
