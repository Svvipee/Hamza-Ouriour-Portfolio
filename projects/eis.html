Mission & constrained selection

Europa Clipper imaging requires science-target prioritization within planetary flyby windows. During my internship, I developed autonomous imaging optimization algorithms for the Europa Imaging System and refined frame-selection behavior.

The existing portfolio identifies this mission-planning tool as SIMPLEX and describes the tradeoff between regional coverage, resolution, and future close-up targeting opportunities.

My contribution

  • Developed and refined decision heuristics for autonomous imaging optimization.
  • Improved reported frame-selection efficiency by approximately 23%.
  • Contributed to pre-launch validation and performance testing, working with mission scientists and software engineers on simulated flyby scenarios.

The work described here is my algorithm and validation contribution. It does not imply ownership of the complete EIS instrument or flight software system.

Balancing selection efficiency with science coverage

During simulated-scenario review, an efficient imaging sequence underrepresented a science target. I investigated how the decision heuristics prioritized candidate frames and identified the selection tradeoff that was reducing useful target coverage.

I refined the heuristic and input parameters, then compared the revised sequence with the baseline in collaboration with the science team. The scenario review checked that improved selection behavior preserved the intended science coverage. This work sat within the broader optimization effort that improved frame-selection efficiency by approximately 23%.

Validation & result

The résumé reports ~23% better frame-selection efficiency and participation in performance testing under simulated flyby scenarios. It does not define the efficiency denominator, comparison dataset, or uncertainty, so this figure is presented as the reported relative result, without translating it into an unverified number of extra images.

Engineering takeaway: optimization needs a mission-relevant objective. A faster or more selective algorithm still needs science-team validation of the plan it produces.
