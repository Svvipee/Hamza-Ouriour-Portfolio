V6 engine CAD assembly.

A SolidWorks practice assembly exploring component relationships, model organization, clearances, and kinematics.

CAD practice project. The available artifacts are assembly renders; manufacturing, combustion performance, and physical engine testing are not documented.

Assembly reasoning

The original project focused on understanding a complex multi-part assembly and the interaction of its components. Clearance and kinematic relationships are useful design checks, but rendered geometry alone cannot establish tolerance, strength, or operating performance.

Next evidence: add an assembly tree, a section view through the cranktrain, and a documented interference or motion study.
